# Compliance & risk (get proper advice: this is a checklist, not legal advice)

Before charging anyone:
- [ ] Ask a Kenyan lawyer / the Capital Markets Authority whether your service (news aggregation + summaries + "insights") needs a licence, and where the line sits between information and investment advice.
- [ ] Data licensing: confirm redistribution rights for prices and for any news content.
- [ ] Data protection: if you collect user data (emails, phone numbers), check obligations under Kenya's Data Protection Act (registration, consent, retention).
- [ ] Terms of service + clear disclaimer: information only, no recommendations, no guarantees, past performance is not predictive.
- [ ] Marketing: no promises of returns; no "guaranteed" language; disclose your own positions.

Product guardrails
- Show source and timestamp on every item.
- Mark AI-generated summaries as such; link to the original.
- Never present a model score as a buy/sell call.
- Keep an error-correction process (a visible way to report a wrong item, fixed within 24h).
