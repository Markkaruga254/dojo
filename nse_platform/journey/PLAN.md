# Public trading journey: plan and rules

**Starts after your assessments (after mid-Feb), not before.** It is content and proof-of-use, never investment advice.

## Rules (publish them on day 0)
1. Real money, an amount you can afford to lose completely. State the starting amount only if you are comfortable (a percentage view is fine).
2. Pre-commit your rules in writing before day 1: universe, max position size, stop-loss/exit rules, fees assumed.
3. Log **every** trade in `TRADE_LOG_TEMPLATE.csv`, including fees and taxes (brokerage, exchange/CMA levies, withholding tax where applicable; check current rates).
4. Show losses as prominently as wins. No deleting posts.
5. No "buy this" calls. Say what *you* did and why, and that it is not advice. Disclose positions in anything you post.
6. Report the benchmark (e.g. NASI) next to your return.
7. Define "realised profit" as realised P&L **after fees and taxes**. Do not call unrealised gains "profit".

## Format
- Daily 60–90 s video or a short written log: *What the platform told me → what I did → what happened.*
- Weekly summary: P&L after costs vs benchmark, 3 lessons, 1 product fix prompted by using it.
- Day 30: honest write-up (what worked, what did not, whether you would pay for the product).

## Product proof checklist
- Screenshots of the product moment that informed each decision
- A bug/feature list generated by your own usage (ship at least 3 fixes during the month)

## Ethics & safety
- Do not trade on non-public information. Do not promote a stock you hold without disclosing it.
- Check `../COMPLIANCE.md` before launch.
