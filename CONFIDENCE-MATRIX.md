# Confidence matrix

Use this at the beginning, at each roadmap gate, and at the end. Confidence alone is not evidence: attach a problem, test, pull request, project write-up, or mock retrospective wherever possible.

## Rating scale

| Score | Meaning | Evidence expected |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not studied | No attempt yet |
| 1 | Familiar | Can describe the idea or follow an example |
| 2 | Practising | Can complete a similar task with occasional hints |
| 3 | Independent | Can solve, test, explain trade-offs, and debug without a walkthrough |

## Review questions

At every checkpoint, answer in writing:

1. What can I now do without a walkthrough?
2. Which result proves it?
3. What still requires a hint or copied example?
4. What is the smallest next exercise that would close the gap?
5. What will I re-test in 3–7 days?

Do not average away a critical weakness. A low score in a role-relevant skill should produce a specific practice task, not a longer study day.
