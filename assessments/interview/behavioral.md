# Behavioural bank (STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result)
Write 6 stories now, each reusable for several questions: (1) a project you led, (2) a hard bug, (3) a disagreement or feedback you acted on, (4) learning something fast, (5) a failure and what changed, (6) teaching or helping others (Cloud Track).
Questions: tell me about yourself (60-s pitch) · why this company/role · biggest technical challenge · a time you were wrong · working under a deadline · handling ambiguity · what you do when stuck · how you prioritise · questions to ask the interviewer.
Pitch skeleton: who you are → what you have built (2 specifics) → what you are learning → why this role.
