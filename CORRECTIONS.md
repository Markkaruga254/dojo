## Learning status

- This is a personal practice programme, not a certification or a guarantee of employment.
- A passing test suite covers only the tests that actually ran; it does not prove mastery of every track.
- The ML tokenizer and attention exercises include intentionally incomplete starter functions. Their TODOs are part of the learning work and must not be described as finished implementations.
- The Hugging Face chapter-to-lab map is being verified. Consult `docs/HF_SYLLABUS_CHECK.md` and the official Hugging Face course before relying on a chapter number or completion claim.
- Adapt the schedule if it conflicts with sleep, health, work, or study obligations.

## Project boundaries

- **NSE market insights:** educational engineering work, not investment advice. Data, sentiment, and model output can be incomplete, delayed, biased, or wrong. Preserve source attribution and provider terms; do not imply guaranteed returns.
- **Cloud exercises:** resources may cost money. Confirm current prices and quotas, use least privilege, and delete resources when finished.
- **Security exercises:** practise only in environments you own or are explicitly authorised to test.
- **Third-party resources:** courses, text, images, code, and videos remain subject to their own licences. This repository does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

When correcting a course claim, record the affected path, evidence/source, change made, and whether learners need to repeat an exercise.
